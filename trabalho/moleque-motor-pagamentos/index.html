Project Overview

The Payment Engine is Moleque's payments core. In 2023 I led the legacy modularisation: I purged the platform's dead prototypes (zougue, ingá, archaeology, steelix, rtbl, datatrail) and migrated the Formiguinhas Service namespaces to src/main, reapplying on develop what was worth keeping. I also designed and implemented a POC porting the engine to the Polylith architecture (isolated components and bases) — complete on its branch, and later absorbed by the team in another shape. I developed and tested the Onyx + Datomic integration for asynchronous tasks — peer group orchestrated via Summon, job resume-points recorded in the database — reaching green tests; the integration was removed from the main line before production use.

Architecture & Implementation

The engine's modelling is fact-oriented: Hodur generates the Datomic schemas, Pathom 3 resolves ~284 attributes through decoupled resolvers, and DatalogX implements commands and calculated attributes with a rules engine (Clara Rules). The Onyx integration used a Summon-orchestrated peer group and resume-points persisted in Datomic. The Polylith POC organised the code into bases/ and components/ — the structure never reached develop; the definitive modularisation came in 2026, authored by the team.
