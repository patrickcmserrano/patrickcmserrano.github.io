Rohana

Multi-Tenant Conversational AI Engine for SMEs

Rohana is a multi-tenant Clojure engine for conversational AI agents with tenant-specific RAG, grounding, and guardrails via EDN files. The product is sold under the Daelaam brand; the end client talks to the business's own persona. Messages flow through a local event bus built on Aeron IPC, with service over WhatsApp and Telegram.
